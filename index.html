Brand Philosophy

Andy Warhol
would approve.

A society where even police must hide their equipment. Safety commodified. Printed in vivid orange and blue — because the truth deserves good design.

The dilemma
is the product.

Undercover but instantly accessible. Invisible but always ready. CARROT. makes the paradox physical.

"Stationery by day. Equipment when needed.
No switching required — it was always both."
